# Review and release decisions

Review the qualification commit, its manifest, source lock, identity graph,
measurement contract and proposed protocol. These are author checks; this round
does not claim independent scientific acceptance.

1. **Identity audit:** replay the graph and sample edges against original tables.
   Resolve missing Broad donor mappings, Sanger related-model exclusions and unknown
   engineered status. Confirm that grouping is not based only on model count.
2. **Source/assay audit:** pin exact external matrices and their source-specific
   terms. Approve gene/feature semantics, score comparability and interpretation of
   shared upstream processing. Keep manuscript and 26Q3 versions distinct.
3. **Scientific/statistical design:** decide a useful scope and endpoint, eligibility,
   meaningful effect, precision/power, comparators and multiplicity. Record prior
   literature exposure and do not represent public evidence as fully unseen.
4. **Owner and capacity:** obtain explicit consent, responsibilities and review
   availability. None is currently appointed.
5. **Independent trial:** have unrelated contributors complete bounded tasks and
   measure artifact usefulness and review cost. First-command checks do not satisfy
   this gate.

The decision to approve a preparation/replication trial is distinct from approving
an external predictive claim or opening broad public intake. Reviewers can approve
specific metadata/development tasks while keeping new outcome evaluation closed.
A qualified owner may accept a reproducible negative result without asserting a
prediction improvement. Complete a new outcome freeze only when all necessary
scientific and data conditions have actually been met.

Return reviewed commit/hash, conflicts/prior involvement, commands, findings,
requested corrections, permitted task scope, review capacity and decision
(revise / approve bounded preparation trial / approve frozen evaluation / defer).
Keep unmet gates explicit in `review.json` and `discover/qualification.json`.

No external message is sent by this package. Repository instructions require
separate authorization for outreach/publication, and the user has not supplied an
owner or instructed us to contact the shortlist.
