# Scientific-owner brief — unsent

Discovery@Home is preparing a small contributor pilot on the reliability of compact
cancer-cell dependency predictors across public datasets. We have working model
certificates, a development-only task surface, and reproducible metadata audits.
We need a functional-genomics researcher to choose a useful question and own
scientific acceptance, with statistical support for evaluation design.

## Concrete invitation draft

Would you review a bounded pilot asking whether compact dependency predictors
retain useful information across public model datasets, and whether independent
contributors can produce reusable replication or robustness artifacts?

Our initial model-improvement trial failed its primary held-out gate and did not
establish an advantage over the stronger restricted-panel linear baseline. We now
have provisional metadata pools of 129 Broad NextGen models and 148 Sanger
organoids representing 144 recorded identity components. These are not yet eligible
confirmation cohorts. Assay compatibility, remaining donor mappings, target
support and evaluation design need review.

We would initially ask for a two-hour package review and, if the question is useful,
an agreed review slot during a small invitation pilot. A possible starting budget
is one hour per week for four weeks; this is a proposal, not a commitment anyone
has made. You would be free to narrow the research question or recommend deferral.

The intended outputs include identity/feature-contract corrections, reproducible
comparisons, and well-supported negative findings. Please identify a concrete
research use for at least one of these outputs. No clinical claim, experimental
commitment or automatic authorship is implied.

## Decisions and responsibilities

The scientific owner approves task relevance, acceptable claims, assay comparisons
and biological interpretation. A statistical reviewer approves support, power,
uncertainty and adaptation controls. One person may cover both roles if qualified;
independent computational replay still needs someone other than the artifact author.
The project coordinator handles packaging, task routing, checks and credit records.
Define who can accept a contribution, expected turnaround, capacity and escalation
before opening intake. The owner must explicitly consent to being listed.
