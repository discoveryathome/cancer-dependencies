# Research context for the expanded program

Published dependency prediction precedes this pilot. Shi et al. (2024),
https://doi.org/10.1038/s43018-024-00789-y, supplies a broader reference context.
An October 8, 2026 study by Fırat and Kılıçaslan explicitly evaluates expression-model
transfer to Broad and Sanger organoids and reports weak transfer with uncertainty
about improvement beyond lineage (https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42883455/;
https://doi.org/10.3390/biology15191782). We have inspected reported methods and
results, not reproduced their method. Cross-paper scores cannot be compared as
leaderboard entries because targets, cohorts and fitting procedures differ.

Consequently neither “expression predicts dependencies” nor “2D-to-organoid transfer
is difficult” is a new general discovery here. A valuable result must identify a
specific advance in predictive reliability, coverage, measurement burden or a
well-supported explanation, and ultimately test its intended practical use.

This round's wider ridge baseline is a controlled breadth experiment, not a
published-method reproduction:300 training-selected genes, four ridge penalties,
all 12 inherited targets, shared-fold panel30 reference. Reproducing a suitable
published method remains a separate open task. Full-profile identities, assay
comparability, independent confirmation and scientific ownership remain unresolved.
